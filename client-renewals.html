RENEWAL SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about renewing your insurance policy.

When should I renew my insurance policy?

It is recommended to begin the renewal process before your policy expiry date. This helps reduce the risk of a coverage interruption.

Can I modify my coverage during renewal?

Available coverage changes depend on your insurance provider and policy conditions. Review the available options before confirming.

How can I receive my renewed policy document?

Once your renewal is successfully processed, the updated policy document will be made available through the applicable delivery method.

What happens if my policy expires?

Your coverage may stop or be affected after expiry. Renewal after expiry may involve additional conditions or inspections depending on the insurer and policy type.
