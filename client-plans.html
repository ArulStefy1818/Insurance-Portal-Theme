Frequently Asked Questions.

A few helpful answers to get you started with your insurance plan exploration.

How do I choose the right insurance plan?

Consider your financial responsibilities, coverage requirements, budget and future goals. Review the policy benefits, exclusions and conditions before selecting a plan.

Can I explore multiple insurance categories?

Yes. You can explore health, life, motor and family protection categories to understand the different types of coverage available.

Are the premiums displayed final?

No. Any sample premiums displayed on this page are illustrative. Actual premiums depend on factors such as coverage, eligibility, policy terms and insurer underwriting.

Can I get help before selecting a plan?

Yes. You can contact our support team for assistance understanding plan features, policy conditions and available options before making a decision.
